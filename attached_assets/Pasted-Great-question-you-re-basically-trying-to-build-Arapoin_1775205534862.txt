# ✅ 1. Standard KYC Requirements for Businesses (Developer / API Users)

In fintech, onboarding a **business (B2B developer)** is called **KYB (Know Your Business)** — an extension of KYC.

## 🔹 A. Company Information (Basic KYB)

These are mandatory:

* Legal Business Name
* Registration Number (CAC in Nigeria)
* Business Type (LLC, Sole Prop, etc.)
* Date of Incorporation
* Business Address
* Country of Operation

👉 This is part of **Customer Identification Program (CIP)** ([Veriff][1])

---

## 🔹 B. Directors & Owners (UBO – Ultimate Beneficial Owners)

* Full name of directors
* Percentage ownership
* Date of birth
* Nationality
* Government ID (NIN, Passport, Driver’s License)

👉 Required for **risk assessment & anti-money laundering checks** ([Apriorit][2])

---

## 🔹 C. Business Documents Upload

* CAC Certificate
* Memorandum & Articles of Association
* Tax Identification Number (TIN)
* Utility Bill (proof of address)
* Bank Account Details

👉 Proof of address + document verification is a **core KYC requirement** ([azakaw][3])

---

## 🔹 D. Business Use Case (VERY IMPORTANT FOR APIs)

* What will you use Arapoints API for?
* Expected volume (daily/monthly requests)
* Target customers (individuals, banks, fintechs, etc.)
* Source of funds / revenue model

👉 This is part of **Customer Due Diligence (CDD)** ([Veriff][1])

---

## 🔹 E. Compliance & Risk Data

* Politically Exposed Person (PEP) check
* Sanctions screening
* AML declaration
* Data usage agreement

---

## 🔹 F. Technical Information (Developer-specific)

Since Arapoints is API-based:

* Company website
* App name (if any)
* API use case (NIN, BVN, verification, etc.)
* Callback/webhook URL
* IP whitelist (optional)
