Across Nigeria, people need help every day. Help retrieving a NIN slip. Help checking a WAEC result. Help getting an IPE clearance for a new job. Real problems, with real urgency.

Arapoint connects those people to a network of trained agents — and that agent could be you.

Become an Arapoint agent and you join a community of Nigerians earning while serving their community. You pick a specialization that fits you. You log in. Real jobs appear in your feed. You claim what you can deliver, and you get paid for the ones you complete.

Four specializations are open. Identity covers NIN, BVN, and related verifications. Education handles WAEC, NECO, JAMB, NABTEB, and NBAIS. CAC focuses on company registration and post-incorporation work. A-two-C handles civic services like IPE clearance and birth attestation.

The job feed runs in real time. New requests stream in from across the country. You see the service requested, the customer reference, and the SLA window before you ever press claim. Claim a job, and the timer starts. Process it through your trusted partner channel. Mark it complete with proof, and the customer gets their result instantly.

Every closed job credits a commission straight into your Arapoint Wallet. The amount per service is fixed and visible inside the app, so you always know the rate before you commit. The harder you work, the more you earn — and your pay arrives the same day, withdrawable to any Nigerian bank.

The SLA timer keeps everyone honest. Customers get the speed they were promised. Agents get a fair, transparent track record. Reliable agents see more jobs, sooner.

If you have the skill, the patience, and the integrity to serve, Arapoint will give you the platform.
