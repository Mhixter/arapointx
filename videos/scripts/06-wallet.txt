Most apps make you pay separately for everything. A different card here, a different transfer there. Receipts everywhere.

Arapoint works differently. One wallet, every service.

Your Arapoint Wallet sits at the centre of the platform. NIN slips, BVN retrievals, IPE clearance, birth attestation, WAEC and NECO verifications, JAMB services, exam-checker PINs — all paid from the same balance.

Funding it is the easy part. Every Arapoint account comes with its own dedicated virtual bank account, hosted with trusted Nigerian payment partners like Palmpay, Moniepoint, and Payvessel. Send money from your usual bank app, and your Arapoint balance updates the moment the transfer settles. No upload-receipt drama. No screenshots.

Once funded, you spend across the entire Arapoint catalogue with a single tap. Every transaction is logged in your wallet history with the exact service, the exact amount, and a real receipt you can download or share.

And here is the part most people love. Arapoint pays you back. Many transactions earn a commission straight into your wallet — small amounts that add up the more you use the platform. The more you transact, the more you earn back. It is fixed, transparent, and visible inside the app at all times.

When it is time to take money out, withdrawal is just as simple. Pick your bank, enter the amount, confirm — and the funds land in your account, with the fee shown clearly before you ever press send.

Every fee is fixed. Every receipt is real. Every commission is logged. No hidden charges, no surprise deductions, no vague promises.
