When the Arapoint API hits its limit, we don't just refuse the call. Retry with exponential backoff — one second, two seconds, four seconds — and most spikes recover in seconds, not minutes.

If you're processing payments or onboarding flows where retries are dangerous, send an X-Idempotency-Key header on the request.
